The possible setting prefixes and their value range are listed below:

Absolute Base: bN b=N [ANY VALUE, Hz]-------Relative Base: b+N b-N [ANY VALUE, Hz]

Phenome Rate: rN r=N [ANY VALUE, Ms]--------Relative Phenome: r+N r-N [ANY VALUE, Ms]

Formant Scale: sN s=N [1.0 = m, 1.7 = f]----Relative Formant: s+N s-N [ANY VALUE, Decimal]

Vibrato Depth: vN [ANY VALUE]---------------Relative Vibrato Depth: v+N v-N [ANY VALUE]

Vibrato Rate: wN [ANY VALUE]----------------Relative Vibrato Depth: w+N w-N [ANY VALUE]

Tremolo Depth: mN [ANY VALUE]---------------Relative Tremolo Depth: m+N m-N [ANY VALUE]

Tremolo Rate: nN [ANY VALUE]----------------Relative Tremolo Depth: n+N n-N [ANY VALUE]

Spectral Tilt: tN [ANY VALUE, lower=darker]-Relative Spectral Tilt: t+N t-N [ANY VALUE]

Vocal Effort: gN [0-1, higher=tenser]-------Relative Vocal Effort: g+N g-N [0-1]

Breathiness: hN [0-1]-----------------------Relative Breathiness: h+N h-N [0-1]
